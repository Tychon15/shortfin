# Wallpaper credits

## shortfin-default.jpg

An underwater photograph, shipped so that the wallpaper switcher and the pywal
theming have something to work with on a fresh install.

**This image is not covered by the repository's GPL-3.0 licence.** It is a
third-party photograph — its EXIF records a Canon PowerShot G7 X Mark II, shot
2018-06-04 — obtained from a wallpaper aggregator, and no author or licence
was recorded with it. It is included here for convenience only, and the
photographer is not credited because the source did not name them.

If you own this photograph and want it removed or attributed, please open an
issue and it will be dealt with immediately.

Nothing depends on this particular file. Delete it, drop your own images into
`~/Pictures/Wallpapers`, and everything works exactly the same:

    rm ~/Pictures/Wallpapers/shortfin-default.jpg

The original is 5472x3072; the copy here is scaled to 3840x2160 to keep the
repository small.
